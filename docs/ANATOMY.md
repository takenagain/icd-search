# Anatomy diagrams

Code pages for anatomical references show a schematic body with the relevant region highlighted.

## Artwork, source and licence

| What | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `web/src/anatomy/body.svg`: four views (surface front, back, internal organs, skeleton) | original artwork, generated by `tools/anatomy/gen-body.mjs` (re-run it to regenerate) | **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication) |

Credit is shown under every figure ("Schematic, not to scale. Artwork: Icdex, CC0 1.0") and on the About page.
No third-party images are used, so there is nothing to attribute beyond that and nothing is loaded from Wikimedia or
elsewhere at runtime.

**Deviation from the brief.** Wikimedia Commons or Servier images were suggested. I drew an own CC0 set instead because
highlighting arbitrary regions needs exact, tagged geometry (every highlightable element has `data-t` tags), which
photographs and most third-party illustrations do not offer, and it avoids per-image attribution and share-alike
questions. The trade-off is simpler, schematic drawings. If richer art is wanted later, replace `body.svg` and keep the
tags; record each image's source and licence in the table above and in `ANATOMY_CREDITS`
(`web/src/anatomy/anatomy.ts`).

## How an entry gets a diagram

`anatomyFor()` in `web/src/anatomy/anatomy.ts`, in this order:

1. **Words** in the title, fully specified name or up to 30 index terms, matched with regular expressions, specific organs
   and body parts first (heart, liver, femur, eyelid, …), then generic system words (nerve, bone, skin, hormone).
2. **Body-system chapter** fallback (circulatory, respiratory, digestive, eye, ear, skin, musculoskeletal, genitourinary,
   …) when no word matched.
3. Otherwise no diagram.

The caption states why the region was chosen ("matched “heart” in the title"), so a wrong guess is visible rather than
silent. 55 regions are defined across the four views.

## Coverage (release 2026-01)

Measured with `node web/scripts/anatomy-coverage.mjs` (uses the app's own mapping on the full dataset):

| Group | Entities | With a diagram | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| all entities | 37,211 | 18,012 | 48.4% |
| `XA` anatomy extension codes | 3,506 | 2,387 | 68.1% |
| chapters 03, 05–18 (body-system entries) | | | 100% (by chapter fallback at least) |
| chapter X, extension codes (all) | 14,246 | 1,066 | 7.5% |
| chapters 01, 02, 04, 19–26 | | | 14–90%, whatever words match |

Of the 18,012 matches, 15,291 come from words and 2,721 from the chapter fallback. The 1,119 `XA` codes without a
diagram are mostly muscles, tendons, ligaments, lymph nodes, small vessels, spinal levels and general terms (blood,
embryo, smooth muscle): the drawing has no region for them. Not covered: laterality (left/right), layers within an
organ, muscles, and any entity whose text names no recognised body part in a chapter without a fallback.
